## shark-0.14.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent pixel-art sprite sheet for a 2D side-view underwater survival game. Create a polished small reef SHARK with blue-grey back, pale warm cream underside, muted teal fins, tiny amber eye, recognisable dorsal fin, pointed snout and tail. It must match handcrafted 16-bit farm/life pixel art: restrained 12-colour palette, stepped square pixels, selective dark blue-grey outlines (no black border), no smooth rendering, no gradients, no glows. Canvas exactly 1536 by 1024, a strict invisible 3-column by 2-row grid of 512x512 cells. Exactly six isolated full-body sharks, ALL FACING RIGHT, all the same identity and anatomical proportions. Each shark fits a 400x200 area centred within its cell, nose at same x and body centre at same y, never touching another cell. Row1: swimming tail raised, swimming tail centred, swimming tail lowered. Row2: tense anticipation with gills flared, attack with jaws open but no blood, recoil with closed jaw. Anatomical side view only, no 3D/isometric, no underwater background, no water or bubbles, no labels, numbers, frames, captions, ground shadow or extra objects. Transparent background including gutters. These are actual game assets, readable when each cell is reduced to a 64x64 logical canvas and shown at 2x nearest neighbour. Make strong clean silhouettes and consistent pixel clusters.

## moray-0.14.png

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: actual transparent pixel-art sprite sheet for a 2D side-view underwater survival game. Make one slender MORAY EEL, olive moss green back with warm ochre mottled spots and muted cream belly, small amber eye, continuous low dorsal fin, recognisable long curved tapering eel body. Restrained handcrafted 16-bit farm/life pixel-art palette, square stepped edges and careful pixel clusters, thin selective dark green outlines, no solid black border, no smooth airbrushing, absolutely NO glow or shadow. Canvas exactly 1536x1024, strict invisible 3-column by 2-row grid with 512x512 cells. Exactly 6 full-body moray sprites, ALL FACING RIGHT, consistent identity/proportion and head position. Keep each sprite entirely inside x=48..464 and y=160..352 of its own cell with fully transparent gutters. Row1: swimming body gentle S curve, swimming near straight body, swimming reversed gentle curve. Row2: anticipation curled tail with slightly open mouth, straight lunge with open jaw/no blood, recoil with closed jaw. Anatomical SIDE VIEW, not top-down, not 3D, not a dragon or snake. Each cell will be sampled down to 64x64 logical pixels for a game. No text, numbers, decorations, labels, grid lines, environment, bubbles, shadows or extra objects. True transparent background; only the eel has opaque pixels.
